# 30-60-90 Plan

The shape of a first quarter. Adjust dates to the engagement. The sequence matters more than the exact days.

---

## Days 1 to 30. Understand and stabilize

**Goal:** know what exists, stop the bleeding on anything obviously dangerous, earn trust.

| Workstream | What you do |
|---|---|
| Access and identity | Full picture of users, admins, MFA coverage, dormant accounts |
| Exposure | What is reachable from the internet and should not be |
| Backup | Confirm what is backed up and force one real restore test |
| Incident readiness | Who calls whom. Get the phone list written down |
| Documents | Read every policy that exists. Note the ones that describe a company that no longer exists |
| Interviews | Everyone on the stakeholder map |
| Quick win | Ship one visible improvement |

**Deliverable at day 30:** a short written picture of where things stand. Not the full report. Five pages, plain English, no scores.

**What the sponsor should feel:** "this person understands us, and something already got better."

---

## Days 31 to 60. Decide and prioritize

**Goal:** turn everything you found into a small number of decisions the business can actually make.

| Workstream | What you do |
|---|---|
| Risk register | Build it properly. Plain language, business impact, named owners |
| Prioritization | Rank by risk reduced per dollar and per hour, not by framework order |
| Roadmap | Twelve months, three phases, with what each phase costs |
| Policies | Draft or adapt what is missing. See [Security-Program-Starter](https://github.com/HarrisonWard/Security-Program-Starter) |
| Vendors | Tier them. Review the Tier 1 list. See [TPRM-RightSized](https://github.com/HarrisonWard/TPRM-RightSized) |
| Compliance mapping | Map what you have to whatever framework is driving the deadline |
| Second quick win | Ship another one |

**Deliverable at day 60:** the assessment report and a prioritized roadmap with costs. Present it live. Never send a report cold.

**What the sponsor should feel:** "I know what to do, in what order, and what it costs."

---

## Days 61 to 90. Build the habit

**Goal:** leave behind a program that runs without you, or that a named person can run.

| Workstream | What you do |
|---|---|
| Governance | A recurring meeting that reviews risk and progress. Someone internal chairs it |
| Metrics | Three numbers the board will see every quarter. See [The-Board-One-Pager](https://github.com/HarrisonWard/The-Board-One-Pager) |
| Policies live | Approved, published, and actually communicated to staff |
| Awareness | Something short, useful, and not annoying. See [Security-Lessons](https://github.com/HarrisonWard/Security-Lessons) |
| Incident plan | Written, and tested once with a tabletop. See [Tabletop-Library](https://github.com/HarrisonWard/Tabletop-Library) |
| Ownership | Every open risk has a named internal owner and a review date |
| Board report | First one written, ideally by them with your help |

**Deliverable at day 90:** a working cadence, a current risk register with owners, and a board report they can produce again next quarter.

**What the sponsor should feel:** "we own this now."

---

## The measure that matters

At day 90, ask yourself one question:

> If I disappeared tomorrow, would this program keep running for six months?

If the answer is no, you built a dependency, not a program. Spend the next 30 days fixing that instead of finding more problems.

---

## Adjusting the plan

| Situation | Change |
|---|---|
| Hard compliance deadline | Pull the mapping and evidence work into days 1 to 30 |
| Recent incident | Incident readiness and backup restore move to week one, everything else waits |
| No internal owner exists | Days 61 to 90 become about hiring or appointing one. See [Hiring-Your-First-Security-Person](https://github.com/HarrisonWard/Hiring-Your-First-Security-Person) |
| Plant or OT in scope | Add a separate track. Do not treat it as IT. See [OT-Security-for-IT-People](https://github.com/HarrisonWard/OT-Security-for-IT-People) |
| Insurance renewal inside 90 days | Work backwards from the renewal date. It beats everything else |
