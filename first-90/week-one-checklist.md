# Week One Checklist

Five days. The goal is not to find everything. The goal is to be oriented, be trusted, and have momentum.

---

## Day 1

- [ ] Kickoff meeting held, sponsor present
- [ ] Recap email sent within 24 hours
- [ ] Access request sent to IT with the full list
- [ ] Document request list sent
- [ ] Shared folder created, structure agreed, permissions checked
- [ ] All checkpoint meetings in calendars

## Day 2

- [ ] Read everything they sent in intake, properly
- [ ] Write down the three things that surprised you
- [ ] Write down the five questions you cannot answer yet
- [ ] Whatever access has arrived, log in and check it works
- [ ] Book interviews. All of them, now, even for week four

**Why book everything now:** calendars fill. An interview you book in week three happens in week six.

## Day 3

- [ ] First real look at identity. Users, admins, MFA state
- [ ] Count the admin accounts. Count the ones belonging to people who left
- [ ] Look at what is exposed to the internet
- [ ] First one to one with the internal owner. Ask what they are not saying in group meetings

**Why identity first:** it is fast, it is where incidents start, and it usually produces the first easy win.

## Day 4

- [ ] Two or three stakeholder interviews
- [ ] Walk the environment with the IT lead. Let them talk, do not lead
- [ ] Ask the quiet technical person what worries them
- [ ] Start the risk register. Rough, private, not shared yet

## Day 5

- [ ] Pick the quick win. Something you can deliver in week two
- [ ] Send the first weekly status
- [ ] Note anything blocked, with the owner named
- [ ] Write your own honest note: is this engagement healthy?

---

## What good looks like at end of week one

- You can describe the environment in five sentences
- You know who decides and who blocks
- You have at least partial access
- You have named a quick win
- The client has heard from you in writing twice

## Warning signs at end of week one

| Sign | What it means | Do this now |
|---|---|---|
| Zero access granted | Nobody is actually sponsoring this | Escalate to sponsor this week, not in week three |
| No documents received | Either they do not exist or nobody was assigned | Ask which it is. Both are findings |
| Interviews will not book | People were not told this was happening | Sponsor needs to send an email. Draft it for them |
| Internal owner has not replied | They were volunteered and are overloaded | Talk to them directly. Then talk to the sponsor about their time |
| Scope already moved | It will move five more times | Fix it in writing this week |

**The rule:** anything wrong in week one gets raised in week one. Problems raised early are project management. The same problem raised in week six is your fault.
