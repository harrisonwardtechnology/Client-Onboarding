## The principle

**Ship something small in week two.** Not the report. Something visible that makes a person's day better or removes a real risk.

Turning on MFA for the finance team. Removing twelve accounts belonging to people who left. Getting a restore test done. Something with a before and after.

**Why:** trust is built by delivery, not by analysis. An engagement that produces nothing visible for six weeks gets quietly written off, no matter how good the eventual report is.
