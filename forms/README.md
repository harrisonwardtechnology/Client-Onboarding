# One-Page Forms

Eight forms. One page each. Each goes to the person who actually knows the answers.

Handing one person a forty question document gets you a half filled document in three weeks. Handing eight people one page each gets you eight answers in three days.

| # | Form | Who fills it | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Executive sponsor](01-executive-sponsor.md) | The person paying for this | 10 min |
| 2 | [IT and systems](02-it-and-systems.md) | Whoever runs IT, or the MSP | 15 min |
| 3 | [Finance and payments](03-finance-and-payments.md) | Controller, bookkeeper, or CFO | 10 min |
| 4 | [People and HR](04-people-and-hr.md) | HR lead or office manager | 10 min |
| 5 | [Legal and contracts](05-legal-and-contracts.md) | Counsel, or whoever signs things | 10 min |
| 6 | [Sales and customers](06-sales-and-customer-requirements.md) | Sales lead or account management | 10 min |
| 7 | [Operations and OT](07-operations-and-ot.md) | Plant, facilities, or field ops | 15 min |
| 8 | [Software and engineering](08-software-and-engineering.md) | Engineering lead | 15 min |

**Skip what does not apply.** A five person consultancy does not need form 7. A company with no product does not need form 8.

## How to send them

One email. All the forms attached. One deadline. Name the person for each form in the email body so nobody has to guess.

> Subject: Five to fifteen minutes each, by [date]
>
> Hi all, I am starting the security work with [company] this week. Attached are short forms, one page each, one per area.
>
> - [Name], form 1
> - [Name], form 2
> - ...
>
> Please send them back by [date]. **"I do not know" is a real answer and is often the most useful one.** Do not create anything or look anything up that takes more than a minute. If a question does not apply, skip it.
>
> Happy to fill any of these in on a fifteen minute call instead if that is easier.

## Rules that make these work

- **Every form fits on one page.** If you add a question, remove one
- **Say "I do not know is a real answer" out loud.** Otherwise people stall trying to look competent, and you get nothing
- **Offer the call option.** Some people will never fill in a form. Fifteen minutes on a call gets better answers anyway
- **Chase on day three, not day ten.** A short nudge halfway works better than a long one at the deadline
