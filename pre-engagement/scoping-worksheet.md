# Scoping Worksheet

Turning discovery answers into hours, a shape, and a price. Fill this in before you quote, not after.

---

## 1. Restate the problem

Three sentences maximum, in their words, not yours.

> The real driver is ________________.
>
> Done looks like ________________.
>
> The hard constraint is ________________.

If you cannot write these three lines, do not quote yet. Go back and ask.

---

## 2. Pick the shape

| Shape | Fits when | Typical length |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery sprint | Environment is unknown, scope is unclear | 1 to 2 weeks |
| Gap assessment | A framework or customer requirement is driving | 2 to 4 weeks |
| Program build | Nothing exists and they need the whole thing | 8 to 16 weeks |
| Fractional CISO retainer | Ongoing ownership, board reporting, steady progress | Monthly, 6 to 24 months |
| Incident support | Something happened, now | Days, then a retainer |
| Point project | One thing, well defined (MFA rollout, vendor review, tabletop) | 1 to 3 weeks |

**Common mistake:** selling a program build when the client only needs a discovery sprint. Sell the small thing first. It builds trust and you scope the real work with real information.

### Uncertainty buffer

Add a percentage based on what you do not know:

| Situation | Buffer |
|---|---|
| You have seen the environment, scope is fixed | 10% |
| Normal, some unknowns | 20% |
| Environment unseen, several stakeholders | 30% |
| OT, legacy, or "we are not sure what we have" | 40%+, or do a paid discovery first |

Say the buffer out loud in the proposal. "This includes a contingency because the environment has not been surveyed" reads as honest, not padded.

## 7. Write the boundaries

Copy these into the statement of work. Adjust as needed.

**In scope:**
- (List every system, site, and framework by name)

**Out of scope:**
- Anything not named above
- Penetration testing or any active testing, unless separately authorized in writing
- Remediation work. This engagement recommends. Implementation is quoted separately
- Certification, attestation, or audit sign off
- Legal advice
- Anything requiring administrative access, unless separately agreed

**Assumptions:**
- Read only access provided by day one
- Named internal owner available X hours per week
- Documents provided within five business days of request
- Interviews scheduled within two weeks of kickoff

**What happens if assumptions break:** the timeline moves day for day and we tell you the same week, not at the end.

---

## 8. Sanity checks before you send

- [ ] Could a stranger read the deliverables list and know what they are getting?
- [ ] Is there a single named decision maker in the document?
- [ ] Did you include the overhead hours, not just the fun hours?
- [ ] Is "out of scope" longer than one line?
- [ ] Would you be happy doing this work at this price if it went 20% over?
- [ ] Did you score it on the go or no-go card first?
