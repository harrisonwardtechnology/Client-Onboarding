# Go or No-Go Scorecard

Score it within twenty four hours of the qualifying call, before you talk yourself into it.

Ten questions. Score each 0, 1, or 2. Twenty points possible.

| # | Question | 0 | 1 | 2 | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Is there a single decision maker? | No idea | A committee | One named person | |
| 2 | Is the driver real and dated? | Vague interest | Real, no date | Real, hard date | |
| 3 | Is budget known and approved? | Secret | Known, not approved | Known and approved | |
| 4 | Is there remediation budget after the assessment? | None | Maybe | Yes | |
| 5 | Will they give internal hours? | Zero | A few, unnamed | Named person, hours agreed | |
| 6 | Will they give read access to what you need? | Resistant | With friction | Yes | |
| 7 | Can they describe what done looks like? | No | Roughly | Clearly, in their words | |
| 8 | Will the sponsor attend the kickoff? | No | Probably | Confirmed | |
| 9 | Is the scope stable? | Moved twice already | Moved once | Fixed in writing | |
| 10 | Do you actually want to do this work? | No | Neutral | Yes | |

**Total: ___ / 20**

## Reading the score

| Score | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 16 to 20 | Good engagement | Scope it, price it normally, get moving |
| 11 to 15 | Workable with conditions | Name the weak scores out loud before signing. Fix them or price them |
| 6 to 10 | Likely painful | Only take it if you can fix two low scores first. Otherwise refer it out |
| 0 to 5 | Walk | Say no warmly and early. Give them two names |

## Automatic no, regardless of score

- They want the assessment to reach a predetermined conclusion
- They want you to attest, certify, or sign off as an auditor
- They ask you to test something without written authorization
- Anything illegal, or anything aimed at a third party

## Conditions to attach when the score is middling

Pick the ones matching your low scores. Put them in the statement of work, not in an email.

- "Work begins after a named internal owner is confirmed in writing"
- "Scope is fixed as written. Changes go through a written change note, priced separately"
- "Read only access to the systems listed in Appendix A is required before day one. Delays move the timeline day for day"
- "This engagement produces recommendations. It does not certify or attest to anything"
- "Sponsor attends kickoff and the four checkpoint calls. If two are missed, we pause and reset"

## A note on question 10

Do not skip it and do not fudge it. Work you dread gets done slower and worse. If the money is good and the answer is still no, the money is not good enough.
