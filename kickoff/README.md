# Kickoff

Week one. One meeting, ninety minutes, everyone who matters in the room.

| File | Use it for |
|---|---|
| `kickoff-agenda.md` | The ninety minutes, minute by minute |
| `stakeholder-map.md` | Who is who, and who actually decides |
| `raci.md` | Who does what, so nothing sits unowned |
| `communication-norms.md` | Cadence, channels, escalation, and the 2am question |

## The rule

**If the sponsor cannot attend, move the meeting.** Do not run kickoff without them. An engagement that starts without its sponsor spends the rest of its life trying to get their attention.
