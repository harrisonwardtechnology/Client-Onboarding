# Stakeholder Map

Who is who, who decides, and who can quietly stop the work.

Fill this in during week one. Update it when you are surprised.

---

## The core table

| Name | Role | What they care about | Influence | Attitude | How they like to be reached |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | High / Med / Low | Champion / Neutral / Blocker | |

**Influence** is not the same as seniority. The person who has been there fifteen years and knows where everything is often has more influence than the new VP.

**Attitude** is not permanent. Blockers usually become neutral once you understand what they are protecting.

---

## Questions to answer about each

- What does success look like **for them personally**?
- What are they worried this project will create for them?
- Have they been through something like this before? How did it go?
- Do they have the time this actually needs, or are they being volunteered?

**Why the second question:** most resistance is not disagreement. It is someone correctly predicting that your project will add work to a job that is already full. Naming that out loud usually defuses it.

---

## Reading the blockers

| What they say | What it often means | What helps |
|---|---|---|
| "We tried this before" | They were burned and nobody acknowledged it | Ask what happened. Listen fully |
| "That will break production" | It might. They have been ignored before | Take it seriously, ask them to help design around it |
| "We do not have time" | True | Cut scope or get the sponsor to free time. Do not argue |
| "That is not how we do things here" | You missed context | Ask what you are missing |
| Silence, missed meetings | They were not consulted before being assigned | Talk to them alone, without their manager |

---

## The rule about going around people

Do not. Escalating over someone's head buys you one decision and costs you every future conversation with that team.

If you must escalate, tell the person first:

> "I am stuck on this and I am going to raise it with [sponsor] on Thursday. I wanted you to hear it from me first. Is there anything you want me to include?"
