# Intake

After signing, before kickoff. This is the homework.

Send all five files at once with a single deadline. Sending them one at a time drags intake out for a month.

| File | What it captures |
|---|---|
| `tech-stack-inventory.md` | What they run and who runs it |
| `compliance-drivers.md` | What actually forces their hand |
| `incident-history.md` | What has already gone wrong |
| `vendor-list.md` | Who else touches their data |
| `data-map-lite.md` | What data they hold and where it sits |

## How to run intake without it dying

- **One email, one deadline, five attachments.** Not a drip
- **Name the owner for each file.** IT owns the stack, finance owns vendors, legal owns compliance. Unassigned forms do not get filled in
- **Say "I do not know is a real answer" in the email.** Otherwise people stall trying to look competent
- **Chase on day three, not day ten.** A short nudge halfway through works better than a long one at the deadline
- **Offer to fill it in on a call.** Some clients will never write it down. Forty five minutes on a call gets you better answers anyway

## What to do with the answers

Read them all before kickoff. Arrive with three specific questions from their own answers. It proves you read it, and it changes how seriously they take the rest of the engagement.
